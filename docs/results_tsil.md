# TSIL scenario — real discovery run

> **V1.1 status: historical / legacy, not V1.1 evidence.**  This run predates
> the global holdout ledger and label-dependency isolation.  Its holdout was
> executed once and is now recorded as consumed; it cannot be re-read or
> re-used as a fresh confirmatory test.  Its rows are `stage=legacy` and its
> isolation cannot be proven, so the conclusions below are `NOT_VERIFIED`
> under the V1.1 protocol.  See
> [the V1.1 remediation report](v1.1_remediation_report.md).

This is the primary scientific scenario of V1.0 and the only TSIL run reported
here.  It is a **compute-limited** run on real data: the upstream TSIL code,
LUSI loss, qlib panel, folds and metrics are unchanged, but the training budget
is reduced (3 epochs / 150 steps per screening trial, 8 epochs / 300 steps per
validation trial, LSTM `d_model=32`).  The conclusion is therefore about
*this* budget and must not be read as reproducing the paper's numbers.

## Feedback loop

Round 1 is not an independent seed sample: it is generated by mutating round-0
evidence.  The only validated round-0 candidate, `volatility(window=10)`, was
the dominant parent; round 1 proposed window mutations (5, 15), feature-slice
mutations (`[0,39]`, `[0,79]`, `[79,158]`) and P-matrix mixes with the native
`phi_volatility` predicate.  Three round-1 mutations validated: the two
`volatility(window=10)` feature slices (`0:39`, `0:79`, both children of
`cand_797f170cfe3d6353`) and one `drawdown(window=20)` feature slice (child of
the *insufficient* `cand_bbc2874e806870b2`).  This shows the feedback step is
doing real work rather than resampling the same template — but it also means
the four validated candidates are **not independent discoveries**: one is the
round-0 volatility candidate itself, two are its children, and the drawdown
one is a borderline child of an insufficient round-0 candidate.  The report should therefore be read as "a
volatility-family predicate improved IC on the search folds under this budget",
not as four separate confirmations.

### Combination exploration

Six composite candidates were generated and executed: one `concat` composite
(momentum+volatility+trend) and five `mix` composites (P-matrix blends of a
discovered descriptor with native `phi_volatility`, or of
`phi_return_dist` with momentum).  None was promoted from the screening fold —
the best screen effects were +0.0030 and +0.0022, below the promoted set —
so no composite reached full validation.  Combination generation and execution
therefore work, but this run provides no validated composite result.

## Frozen final evaluation

The strongest pre-holdout candidate `cand_db0442a12390d0c5` (volatility window
10, features 0:79) was frozen (manifest hash `3244f6a6…c46195`, frozen at
2026-10-31T17:14:48Z) and evaluated once on 2024-01-01..2025-08-31, training on
2018–2022 with 2023 as the early-stopping validation block.  No other candidate
was evaluated on the holdout, so it remains a single confirmatory test.

| model | IC | RankIC | ICIR | MSE | MAE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MSE baseline | 0.05436 | 0.05257 | 3.435 | 0.99485 | 0.86325 |
| volatility 0:79 | 0.05333 | 0.05141 | 3.311 | 0.99490 | 0.86338 |

Paired daily effect over 403 holdout trading days: **−0.00103 IC**
(95% bootstrap CI [−0.00328, +0.00110], HAC p = 0.386, Cohen's dz = −0.042).

**The holdout does not confirm the search-fold result.**  The point estimate
reverses sign and the CI includes zero, so there is no out-of-sample evidence
that this predicate improves IC at this training budget.  The search-fold
effect (+0.0038) was small and did not survive a regime shift to 2024–2025.
This is a genuine negative result, not a mock or a success story: the discovery
and validation machinery worked, but the scientific claim it produced did not
generalise.

Two things this result does **not** show:

* that the platform's statistics are wrong — the search-fold effect was
  computed on four years of data and the holdout is a different regime;
* that volatility predicates are useless in TSIL — the test covers one
  candidate from one family at a reduced budget, not the 23-predicate suite.

It does show why the separation matters: had the holdout been used during
search, the +0.0038 would have looked like a discovery.  Instead the honest
conclusion is "search-fold candidate failed confirmation".

## Honest summary

* The real TSIL loop ran end to end: real qlib data, real LSTM + LUSI loss,
  real baseline/control, 4-fold rolling validation, BH correction, evidence for
  every candidate, a second feedback round, and a frozen holdout evaluation.
* Four candidates met the pre-registered validation bar on the search folds
  (a `volatility(window=10)` family plus one borderline `drawdown` mutation).
* None of that survived the frozen holdout evaluation that was actually run.
* Therefore V1.0 demonstrates a **working discovery-and-validation system**,
  not a validated scientific finding about TSIL predicates.
